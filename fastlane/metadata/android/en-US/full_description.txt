Studio Camera is the companion app for your Studio Camera Box or Bridge. Pair via QR code or NFC, then control your camera wirelessly.

Features:
- Live view with low-latency MJPEG streaming
- Remote camera controls: ISO, shutter speed, aperture, EV, focus
- Grid and safe zone overlays for composition
- Media browser with download and sharing
- Secure TLS connection with device authentication
- mDNS device discovery on your local network
- NFC and QR code pairing
- Dark and light themes

Studio Camera connects to your Studio Camera Box over your local Wi-Fi network. No cloud services or accounts required — your footage stays on your device.
